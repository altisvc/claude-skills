## The Problem

VCs are trapped between three failure modes:
1. **Wasting time** on deals that ultimately die from obvious flaws
2. **Passing on breakouts** they didn't have bandwidth to see
3. **Losing deals** because they couldn't signal conviction fast enough

The status quo forces a tradeoff between depth and breadth. Traditional diligence delivers real depth, but it's slow and costly. Data platforms offer broad coverage, but only surface-level insight. AI tools have only added noise.

What's missing is a fast path to trusted signal: the ability to get deep before going deep and lead with insight.

---

## The Solution

Altis is **intelligence infrastructure for private markets**, delivering institutional-quality diligence on Series A+ companies.

We identify 1–4 key debates that should drive an investment committee decision. Then we build out the bull and bear cases through primary research — interviews with customers, competitors, former employees, and domain experts. Each report culminates in a **What Makes or Breaks a Deal** assessment and the **Altis View** — our editorial synthesis of where the evidence lands.

The result: the diligence depth you'd want on every deal, available when you need it.

---

## How Altis Works

Every Altis report starts with a decision frame: *What are the 1–4 questions that should determine whether this is a yes or a no?*

From there:

1. **We define the key debates.** The questions that actually matter for an IC decision — not a generic SWOT.
2. **We conduct extensive primary research.** Interviews with customers, churned customers, competitors, former employees, and industry experts.
3. **We build the bull and bear cases.** Each key debate gets evidence on both sides, drawn from primary sources.
4. **We assess what makes or breaks the deal.** The structural factors that determine outcome independent of thesis debates.
5. **We deliver the Altis View.** Our editorial synthesis of where the evidence lands — not a hedge, a position.

**What you receive:**
- 1–4 key debates with bull/bear cases
- What Makes or Breaks a Deal assessment
- Altis View (editorial position)
- Primary interview evidence (cited, not summarized)
- Risk flags and open questions

## Core Positioning

**Tagline:** Rise above the noise.

**Elevator pitch:** Altis helps investors *get deep before going deep* — delivering fast, trusted intelligence so they can separate signal from noise early.

**Why we exist:** Venture capital is one of the least information-efficient major asset classes. Information is compounding faster than human judgment can keep up. Altis is the shared research infrastructure the industry has been missing.

**Positioning guardrails (from Ben):** Never lead with Midas List (use as reason-to-believe). Never say "incubated." Never imply VCs are uninformed — the asset class is maturing and infrastructure must mature with it.

---

## What Altis Is NOT

- **Not an AI chatbot** — We don't summarize public information. We conduct original research.
- **Not a consulting engagement** — We don't take weeks. The work is already done.
- **Not a data scraper** — We don't aggregate public filings and news articles. We build original insight from primary sources.

## Output Standards

Every output should help the reader:
- See more clearly
- Decide more confidently
- Focus on what is actually consequential

**If something does not materially improve clarity, cut it.**

Write as if the output could be sent directly to a senior investor. Favor concise bullets, clear structure, and declarative statements.
