07 · Unit economics
The model gets stronger at scale

The model gets stronger as it scales

Three numbers to watch over the four years. Each one moves in our favor because each new customer reuses research the previous customer paid us to build.

Revenue per report
$44K
from $6K in Q4 2026 — ~7x
The same report packaged for twelve buyers earns twelve times the revenue. As coverage deepens, the marginal cost of each new sale drops while average price per report rises.
Gross margin
+60%
from –178% in Q4 2026
Margin turns positive in Q1 2028. Past that point, every additional customer in a covered AUM tier flows almost entirely to gross profit. The Base case ends 2029 at ~60% gross margin.
Hard costs per report
$5,000
from $5,187 in Q1 2026
As coverage compounds, the knowledge graph absorbs more of each new report's work. That density holds hard cost steady against rising expert call and tooling rates.
Compounding research, not sales headcount, is what makes this business work at $50M+ ARR

10 · Path to profitability
Investment phase → operating phase

Breakeven Q2 2029, profitable from there

The company's first three years are the investment phase: build the team, build the coverage, build the agent stack. By 2028, gross margin turns positive. By Q2 2029 (Base) the company runs at breakeven. Upside accelerates that timeline by several quarters.

The four phases, Base case
2026
Build the foundation
Hire research team to 20 FTEs. Ship 189 reports. First 25 paying customers. Gross margin: deeply negative — we are paying to learn.
2027
Reach scale on research
Research grows to 34. Coverage compounds — ship 504 reports for the year. ARR run-rate hits $8.6M. Gross margin still negative but improving every quarter.
2028
Margin turns positive
Gross margin crosses zero in Q1. ARR run-rate hits $18M by year-end. Sales and CS scale up to handle 264 customers. The unit economics finally work.
2029
Breakeven, then profit
416 customers. $27M ARR run-rate. Net income turns positive in Q2. The business runs on its own cash flow from there.
