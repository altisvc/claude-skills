skill: setup
---

Run the Setup skill to configure your Claude Code environment.

This wizard:
1. Asks your name, email, org (Altis or Primary), and role
2. Detects your project directory
3. Probes for available integrations (Slack, Trello, Granola, gws, Thoughtful, GitHub)
4. Writes `.claude/user.yaml` with org defaults + your personal config
5. Recommends skills based on your role and integrations

Re-run anytime to update your config.
