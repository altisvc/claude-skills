## Section 1: Executive Summary

### Market / TAM

- Cloud infrastructure spending reached $270B in 2025, growing 22% YoY.
- Mid-market companies are underserved — 60% lack dedicated DevOps teams.
- TAM: $45B for managed infrastructure, narrowing to $12B for AI-assisted ops.

### Company / Product / GTM

- Founded 2022 by Jane Smith (ex-AWS, 15 years infrastructure). $28M raised through Series A.
- 85 enterprise customers. 3.2x ARR growth. 140% NRR.
- Product automates infrastructure provisioning in under 10 minutes.

### Altis Assessment

Acme has strong founder-market fit and early traction in a large market. Key risk is competitive response from hyperscalers bundling similar capabilities.

## Section 2: Key Debates

### Debate 1: Can Acme defend against AWS and GCP bundling managed ops into their core platforms?

**Bull case:**
- Hyperscalers are slow to ship opinionated tooling for mid-market
- Acme's value is cross-cloud, which no single hyperscaler will build
- Customer lock-in through workflow integrations

**Bear case:**
- AWS has announced "AI Ops" features shipping Q3 2026
- Price compression if bundled at zero incremental cost
- Mid-market customers default to vendor-provided tools
